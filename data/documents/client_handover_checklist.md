# Client portfolio handover checklist

1. List all active clients, joint committees and CAO/CCT agreements.
2. Collect client-specific exceptions (these often live only in email).
3. Identify key contacts at the client and internal experts.
4. Review open tickets and upcoming deadlines (cutoffs, year-end).
5. Schedule a one-hour handover conversation with the previous consultant.
